Frequently asked questions

Direct answers to common professional wrestling questions.

Q.01

What is the difference between WWE and AEW formats?

WWE presents a weekly, scripted sports-entertainment format across brands such as Raw and SmackDown; AEW presents a weekly wrestling format centered on Dynamite and Collision, with its own roster and championship lineages.

Q.02

What are the highest-attended live wrestling events in history?

Frequently cited reported or announced figures place Collision in Korea, WrestleMania 32, and WrestleMania III among the highest-attended events; figures vary by source and counting method.
