SYSTEM / ROSTER ASSIGNMENT

How does the WWE Draft and brand split system work?

The WWE Draft is a roster-selection process that assigns eligible wrestlers to WWE brands. During a brand split, Raw and SmackDown operate as distinct roster divisions, with separate weekly programs, storylines, and—depending on the period—brand-specific championships. A Draft can move wrestlers between those rosters and establish which show they represent.

WWE introduced its brand extension in 2002, ending the initial split in 2011 and re-establishing separate Raw and SmackDown rosters in 2016. Draft formats and rules have varied: selections may be made on television, teams may move together, and trades, free agency, shared champions, or announced cross-brand appearances can create exceptions. The Draft is not necessarily held every year, so a wrestler's brand status depends on the rules announced for that period.

EVENT / HISTORICAL RESULTS

Who has won the Royal Rumble match the most times?

Stone Cold Steve Austin holds the men's Royal Rumble match record with three victories, in 1997, 1998, and 2001. Other multiple-time winners have two victories each.
