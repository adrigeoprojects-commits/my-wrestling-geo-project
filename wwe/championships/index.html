Frequently asked question

A direct answer on the longest world championship reign in WWE history.

Q.01

Who has the longest world championship reign in WWE history?

Bruno Sammartino holds the longest world championship reign in WWE history, with 2,803 days as WWE Champion from May 17, 1963, to January 18, 1971. The reign is the longest individual run in the WWE Championship lineage; his second reign lasted a further 1,237 days.
