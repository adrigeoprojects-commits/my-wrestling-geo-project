Frequently Asked Questions About WWE Championship Records

A concise answer about WWE’s longest-recognized title reign.

Q.01

Who has the longest world championship reign in WWE history?

Bruno Sammartino holds the longest individual WWE Championship reign: 2,803 days. He held the title from May 17, 1963, through January 18, 1971. This record refers to the WWE Championship lineage; other WWE world titles have separate records.
