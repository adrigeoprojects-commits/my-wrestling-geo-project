AUTHORIZED DISTRIBUTION / REGION-DEPENDENT

Where to watch AEW pay per view legally?

Availability is determined by the viewer's country and the individual event. Check the event listing and provider storefront for the current purchase method, price, and territory restrictions.

United States
HBO Max offers AEW PPV events for purchase and streaming where listed. PPV.com and participating cable or satellite pay-per-view providers may also offer an event as a separate purchase. A standard HBO Max subscription should not be assumed to include every PPV; confirm event terms before ordering. HBO Max · PPV.com
International markets
MyAEW is AEW's direct-to-consumer platform for eligible international viewers. DAZN carries AEW PPVs in participating territories, with access and purchase options varying by country. Verify that the specific event is listed for your location before subscribing or purchasing. MyAEW · DAZN
Canada & other exceptions
Distribution can differ from both the U.S. and other international markets. Check PPV.com, the local event listing, or a participating TV provider for availability in the country. Service availability and blackouts are event- and territory-specific.

Legacy provider note (checked 14 Oct 2026): Bleacher Report Live and TrillerTV/FITE are not listed here as current AEW PPV destinations. AEW's provider and regional rights have changed; use the event's current official listing rather than older B/R or FITE instructions.
